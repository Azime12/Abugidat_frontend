"A Day in the Life"

Primary Care Worker

Morning: Syncs schedule and checks for today's visits.
Field: Household visit; QR scan identifies a pregnant mother.
Action: Flags risk; system auto-generates referral code.
Review: Checks clinic feedback from yesterday's patients.

"A Day in the Life"

Health Facility Administrator

09:00 AM: Reviews the "Incoming Referrals" queue.
11:30 AM: Verifies patient arrival on the Web Portal.
02:00 PM: Logs treatment result to notify the PC Worker.
05:00 PM: Exports the weekly City Health performance report.
